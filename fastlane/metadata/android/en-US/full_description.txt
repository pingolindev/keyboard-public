Pingolin Keyboard is an open source keyboard with no internet access. It cannot send what you type anywhere.

• Suggestions, autocorrect and next-word predictions in 23 languages
• Glide typing
• Voice typing on your phone, with a voice model you download once
• Clipboard history, a text editing panel and terminal keys
• Password manager suggestions above the keys, on Android 11 and later
• Incognito mode, on by itself where an app asks for it
• No account, ads or tracking

English (US) is built in. Other languages and voice models are files you get in your browser and open with the keyboard.

The source code, under the GPL, is at github.com/pingolindev/keyboard-public.
